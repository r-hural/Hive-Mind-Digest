## 3. Anchoring and habit stacking

### 3.1 The anchoring principle and the 'after I...' format
- Habit stacking pairs a new habit with an already established one in the form 'after I \[current habit\], I will \[new habit\]'. [Successful By Design]

### 3.2 Examples of anchored habits
- Example stacks include doing 10 push-ups while morning coffee brews and closing the eyes for five deep breaths after getting into the car. [Successful By Design]

### 3.3 Choosing and matching triggers
