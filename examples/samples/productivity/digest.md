He argues that stagnation usually stems from not having the right system built around a person rather than from a personal defect in ability, productivity or motivation. Coordination rests on a catch-all capture system (a notebook and Reminders) and a calendar in Apple Calendar that shows immovable commitments and the baseline of available time. He says that without knowing this time you cannot be productive. Consolidation is handled in Apple Reminders, which holds a catch-all list that tasks are moved from into project lists for jobs, university and YouTube, plus a Today list. Creation uses notebooks in early stages, GoodNotes on iPad, and the Apple filing system, which syncs across devices. The filing system is organised into personal, university and YouTube folders, and he recommends scanning paper notebooks into it. He says the Apple filing system and file storage matter, and suggests an external hard drive for those with limited computer storage. The overall message favours sustainability and simplicity over a large number of productivity apps: the speaker recommends using just a notebook, Reminders, Apple Calendar and built-in tools, and trusting one coherent system. The content is a personal workflow demonstration rather than tested evidence, and the speaker refers viewers to earlier videos on his pocket notebook and time management for more detail.
